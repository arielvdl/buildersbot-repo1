### Changed

- The extension menu has a new look, in the colors of the Builders Bot app, dark or light as your
  system is. The top shows the link state. When Chrome is not connected, one card shows what is
  wrong, with Connect as the main button. Each project is a card with its color, its tabs, its agents,
  and its blocked and allowed sites. Stop all stays at the bottom in every state, with its shortcut.
- The welcome page shows what the extension reads, where it goes, what it never touches and how to
  stop it in four cards. The pairing words are large, and the three choices are large buttons.
