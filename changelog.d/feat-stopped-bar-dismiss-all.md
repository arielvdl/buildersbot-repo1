### Added

- The block of stopped tasks above the message field of a channel has Dismiss on each task. With two
  or more stopped tasks it also has a bar with Continue all and Dismiss all. Dismiss closes the work
  for good, so nothing starts it again, and Undo opens it again for 10 seconds. It shows on this
  computer's server only.

### Fixed

- "Resume by itself" no longer starts again a task that you stopped. Before, it did after its interval.
