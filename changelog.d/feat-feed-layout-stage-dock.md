### Changed

- The Feed page gives most of the screen to the agents. The tables of the projects are larger, and
  the agents grow with the room that they have. The list of runs is a quiet drawer under them, with
  one line for each run. Select List to fold it.
- A run in the list has no frame and no coloured edge. A dot shows its state. A run opens under its
  line only when it needs you, has stopped, or runs slowly.
- The buttons of a run, Stop, Open and Steps, show when the pointer or the focus is on its line. A
  long name of an agent or a project is cut with an ellipsis, and the tooltip shows it in full.
- The Stop button has a solid square. Before, it looked like a checkbox.

### Added

- Stop all, at the top of the Feed page, stops every run that works now. It asks first, and says how
  many runs it ends. A run that cannot stop shows why on its own line.
- The Feed page remembers whether you folded the list, on this computer.
